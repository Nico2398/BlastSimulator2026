# Plan — #2116 (2116-40822508814)

## Contract
- Criteria (sandbox site = TerrainConfig with `oreDeposits: true`, built as `tests/helpers/sandboxSite.ts` `sandboxTerrainConfig(biome, seed)`; campaign levels do not set it):
  - C1 `buildTerrainContext(config).starterDeposit` is a `StarterDeposit` for every biome in {desert_badlands, green_foothills, red_canyon, alpine_granite, tropical_karst, volcanic_flats} x seeds 1..5 (plus 12345 and 42); it is `null` when `oreDeposits` is not true.
  - C2 Deterministic: two contexts/grids from the same config give the same `starterDeposit` and the same voxels (ore + rock) in the deposit's area.
  - C3 Close: the deposit centre lies 10..25 m (horizontal, grid cells = metres) from the site centre (sizeX/2, sizeZ/2), and after `sandbox start` within 5..35 m of the nearest starting building.
  - C4 Reachable: the ground under the deposit's 9x9 shot footprint has surface height spread <= SANDBOX_FLAT_PAD_MAX_SPREAD, and the footprint is climb-connected to the rally point at the vehicle slope limit (no ramp) after `sandbox start` (digger and full kits).
  - C5 Shallow: for every column of the shot footprint centred on the deposit, ore of `starterDeposit.oreId` is present in the voxels at depth 0..2 m below the surface and continues through at least the top 5 m (depth 0..4 m all hold ore, at least in the footprint's holes' columns).
  - C6 Sellable: `oreId` is 'dirtite' or 'rustite'.
  - C7 Breakable: after `sandbox start biome:B kit:K seed:S`, firing the reference shot (3x3 holes, spacing 4, depth 5, Krackle 4 kg each, stemming 2.5 m — see `fireReferenceShot` in tests/integration/sandbox-economy-balance.integration.test.ts, but with the grid origin at (deposit.x-4, deposit.z-4) so the shot is centred on the deposit) through executeBlast clears at least 345 voxels (half of the ~690 of desert_badlands seed 42), for every biome x seeds 1..5.
  - C8 Sized: the same shot yields between STARTER_DEPOSIT_YIELD_KG_MIN and STARTER_DEPOSIT_YIELD_KG_MAX kg (balance.ts, roughly 10 000..20 000) of the deposit's ore in its fragments (ore kg = fragment volume x density x ORE_DENSITY_KG_M3 over fragments), not barren and not all oversized (some fragment of ore is haulable, i.e. not isOversized).
  - C9 Discovery unchanged: the ore is real voxel ore (`grid.oresAt`), nothing else (no extra flags/markers in GameState) marks it.
  - C10 Campaign: a campaign level's terrain (levels 1-5, `oreDeposits` unset) is byte-identical to before: `starterDeposit` null and the existing terrain fingerprint tests stay green.
  - C11 Outside the deposit's pocket, terrain/ore of a sandbox site is unchanged except in the starter pocket's own area (columns farther than STARTER_DEPOSIT_POCKET_RADIUS_M + 1 from its centre generate exactly as before).
- API:
  - `src/core/world/StarterDeposit.ts`: `export interface StarterDeposit { readonly x: number; readonly z: number; readonly oreId: string; readonly radius: number }` (x,z = centre cell; radius = pocket radius m); `export function resolveStarterDeposit(...)` (internal use by TerrainGen).
  - `src/core/world/TerrainGen.ts`: `TerrainContext.starterDeposit: StarterDeposit | null`.
  - `src/core/config/balance.ts`: `STARTER_DEPOSIT_MIN_DISTANCE_M`, `STARTER_DEPOSIT_MAX_DISTANCE_M` (10, 25), `STARTER_DEPOSIT_POCKET_RADIUS_M`, `STARTER_DEPOSIT_YIELD_KG_MIN`, `STARTER_DEPOSIT_YIELD_KG_MAX` (the band the run measures and states), `STARTER_DEPOSIT_MIN_CLEARED_VOXELS` = 345.
- Edge cases: `oreDeposits` false/absent -> null and no change; border zone stays ore-free; a seed whose centre biome is hard-rock still gets the soft pocket.
- Tests go in: tests/unit/world/StarterDeposit.test.ts, tests/integration/sandbox-starter-deposit-2116.integration.test.ts

## Approach
- StarterDeposit.ts picks centre by scanning candidate cells on a ring 10..25 m from site centre, scored by 9x9+ footprint height spread then seeded tie-break (subSeed), using sampleSurfaceHeightY; oreId chosen from seed between dirtite/rustite (rustite default fits sandbox contracts).
- TerrainGen.generateColumnRange: inside the pocket, composition = weathered soft rock (softest catalog rock, ids from RockCatalog), ore = fixed density profile of oreId down to pocket depth, scaled by gradeScale like other ore. Not in border zone. Core never adds ore after gen.
- Balance constants calibrated by a measurement script; yield band stated in PR.
- Channels owed: static, logic, scenario, probe matrix, visual (seismic survey screenshot on alpine_granite).
