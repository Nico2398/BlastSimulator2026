# Issue #1922: Progression baseline (measured)

Measured baseline of the early-game progression probe, refreshed after the reinvest
fix (#1974: reload gate at 50% of the last blast's yield, cash gate, buy fall-through).
Nothing was retuned; this is what the game does today.

## Rerun

```bash
npm run long -- start probe -- npm run probe:progression -- --json <path>
npm run long -- wait probe      # explicit timeout 600000; exit 75 = still running, call again
npm run probe:progression -- --quick   # reduced matrix, fast sanity check
```

- Base commit: `main` at `0d0c0223` (the #1974 reinvest fix on top). The first baseline was taken at `f441aa52`.
- Matrix: 3 seeds (42, 7, 13) x 2 biomes (desert_badlands, green_foothills) x 2 kits (digger, full) x 2 strategies (opening, reinvest) = 24 runs, horizon 10000 ticks.
- Wall time: about 81 minutes on a CI sandbox, sequential (the slowest run, green_foothills/full/seed 42/reinvest, took about 22 min).
- Raw JSON was written to `/tmp/probe-after.json` (not committed).

## TL;DR

- 12 of 24 runs end in bankruptcy before tick 10000 (6 `opening`, 6 `reinvest`), all on seeds 7 and 13; seed 42 survives in all 8 configurations. Bankruptcy ticks range from 553 to 7179. Seed variance dominates every other axis. See "Bankruptcy diagnosis".
- Survivors reach about $2.2M to $6.7M at t6000 and $3.3M to $9.6M at t10000. green_foothills/digger/42 still falls after t6000 ($4.3M to $3.3M opening, $4.3M to $3.7M reinvest).
- First blast sold at t6000 is 5% to 17%, mostly 9% to 13%. Warehouse fills between tick 605 and 6553. Haul waits on full storage reach 6051 ticks.
- `reinvest` now re-fires, but only where the mine survives long enough: 2 of 12 runs fired a second blast (desert_badlands/digger/42 and green_foothills/digger/42; 2 blasts vs 1 for `opening`). All other `reinvest` runs fired 1 blast. Where it re-fires, cash is not better: desert/digger/42 is $6.07M at t10000 vs $6.78M opening, green/digger/42 $3.68M vs $3.29M. Grades: `g1` now at t4153 (desert) and t2185 (green_foothills), against t9293 and t4425 before the fix; no run reached a second grade.
- `opening` is the control and is unchanged. Its rows moved by a constant -$250 against the first baseline (for example cash@1000 467712 to 467462); that offset was not investigated.

## Comparison with #1909's hand figures

#1909 estimated about $8M at t6000 without reinvestment and about a quarter of the first blast sold by then.

Measured desert_badlands/digger/seed 42/opening: $4,948,347 at t6000 and 12% of the first blast sold (JSON: `cashAt["6000"]` = 4948347.36, `firstBlastSoldShareAt6000` = 0.1228). Same order of magnitude, but about 0.6x on cash and about half on sold share. Other survivors sit lower still (desert/full: $5.5M and $2.3M; green_foothills: $2.2M to $4.3M).

Likely causes, not verified by the probe:

- Explosive stocking cost: the simulation pays for explosive stock up front, which the hand figure probably did not charge; it also fits the early cash dips (low points near ticks 430 to 600).
- Contract check cadence: the probe's strategies evaluate contracts on a fixed interval, so sales lag the moment ore becomes available. This also depresses sold share at t6000.
- Single-blast economy: with one blast fired, income is capped by that blast's ore; the hand figure may have assumed steadier supply.

No constant was changed.

## Bankruptcy diagnosis

12 of 24 runs go bankrupt: 6 `opening`, 6 `reinvest`; 6 on seed 7 and 6 on seed 13 (the desert_badlands/full/13 and green_foothills/full/7 runs take 3642 to 7179 ticks; the rest fail between ticks 553 and 2339). Seed 42 never does. The `reinvest` fix did not change the count.

Cause (structural, seed-dependent): time-to-first-income exceeds the payroll runway.

- Payroll is about $275 per tick from t0. Cash is about $166k at t50 and about $46k at t400.
- The first sale waits until stored ore covers a whole contract offer, so income starts only after the first blast is drilled, fired, hauled and stored.
- Seed 42 gets there: first sale around t480, cash trough about -$2k (desert_badlands/digger/42 low -$2382 at t484), then it recovers.
- Seed 7 does not: cash goes negative around t590 and the run is bankrupt at t649 (desert_badlands/digger/7: low -$27,151 at t528, bankrupt t644 in the refreshed run).
- Seed 13 digger runs go bankrupt at t553 (green_foothills) and t1369 to t1919 (desert_badlands).

Verdict (decision taken): `opening` stays unchanged as the control; no opening adaptation was attempted in this run. The failure is structural and depends on the seed's ore layout. Calibration tasks should treat it as a finding about the opening, not retune balance constants to hide it.

Not re-measured: the figures for payroll per tick, cash at t50 and t400, and the first-sale reasoning come from the earlier diagnosis and were not re-derived from this JSON. The "Comparison with #1909's hand figures" section is carried over from the first baseline; its seed 42 opening numbers moved by -$250 only, and its likely causes remain unverified.

## Metric caveats

- `boulderSoldShare` is a proxy (sold share of boulder material), not a per-boulder trace. It is `-` for digger kits.
- `affordableTick` is 1 in every run: start cash exceeds every catalog cost (warehouse T2, research center, second hauler), so it carries no information at the current balance.
- Blasts per strategy: `opening` fires 1 blast in all 12 runs. `reinvest` fires 2 in 2 of 12 (desert_badlands/digger/42 and green_foothills/digger/42) and 1 in the other 10: 6 of those 10 go bankrupt first, and the 4 survivors are full-kit runs where the reload gate (50% of last blast yield) was not reached before the horizon. A grade was reached only in the 2 two-blast runs (`g1@4153` desert, `g1@2185` green_foothills; both digger/seed 42/reinvest); no run reached a second grade. Grade progression is still barely exercised.
- Median rows mix survivors and bankrupt runs per column; read `bankrupt` and `end` before trusting a median.
- Runs that go bankrupt early leave `-` in later cash columns.

## Known deviations

Where the probe's numbers differ from what their column names suggest. None changes the baseline above; each is documented in the code.

- `low cash` / `low tick` is the global minimum of cash over the whole run, not strictly the opening's dip. A later dip below the opening's low point replaces it.
- `boulderSold` is an upper-bound proxy: boulder ore that no longer lies as a boulder (broken, hauled or sold). Broken pieces are new fragments and the probe keeps no lineage, so broken counts as sold. 100% therefore carries little information.
- Median rows are taken over the non-null runs of each column only. A column's median can rest on fewer than 3 runs (bankrupt runs leave `-`), and the `-` cells in a median row mean no run had a value.
- `reinvest` reload policy: the next blast is ordered once haulable ore on the ground falls under 50% of the last blast's yield (`RELOAD_FRACTION`), the idle gate holds (no drill or charge work queued) and cash covers the cost; when a purchase cannot be afforded it falls through to the next one. Calibration tasks extend the policy.
- `--quick` runs both strategies (2 rows) on the single quick site unless `--strategy` is given.
- Deposit ranking reads ground-truth ore densities from the voxel grid, not a survey estimate. The probe does not model surveying.
